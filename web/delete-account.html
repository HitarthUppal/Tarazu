Delete your Tarazu account

Tarazu accounts belong to a company workspace and are created by that company's administrator.

To delete your account, either:

  1. ask your company's Tarazu owner or administrator to remove you (Admin, People, Remove), which takes effect immediately and signs you out of every device; or
  2. email privacy@tarazu.live from the email on your account, or with your registered mobile number, asking for deletion.

What is deleted: your name, mobile number, email address, PIN, push tokens and signed-in sessions.

What is kept: records you entered for your company (views, comments, decisions, sales) stay with the company as business records, shown as a removed user. Encrypted backups containing your details are overwritten within 30 days.
