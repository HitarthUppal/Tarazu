Sugar Sale Intelligence System

Sell sugar at the right moment, not after it has passed.

Tarazu brings a mill's sale offers, an advisory panel's views and market intelligence from ChiniMandi and AgriMandi into one clear answer for the owner: what to sell, at what price, and by when.

Waiting has a price

Every day sugar sits unsold while the advice behind it goes stale, the mill pays for it. Tarazu exists to cut that opportunity cost.

Interest and storage

Stock in the godown ties up working capital and adds storage charges every single day.

Quota that lapses

Unsold monthly quota cannot be carried forward. Miss the window and the sale is gone.

Advice that expires

A view on prices given last week may no longer hold. Decisions made on old advice are guesses.

How it works

Three roles, one AI brief, and a clear deadline at every step.

  1. Mill managerPosts a round

    Price, quantity, grade, stock in hand and quota remaining, for each mill.

  2. Advisory panelGives independent views

    Each advisor answers separately and says how long their advice holds.

  3. Tarazu AIAdds the market picture

    A brief built on ChiniMandi news, AgriMandi reports and the mill's own sales history. It adds context, it never decides.

  4. OwnerDecides, on time

    One answer card with the recommendation, the deadline and the daily cost of waiting.

What you get

Nothing in Tarazu is there for looks. Every screen opens with the action, then the deadline, then the reason, then the data.

Action items with Act by

Each person sees what needs doing next, soonest deadline first.

Cost of waiting

Interest and storage per day, worked out from your own rates, so delay has a number.

An advisory panel that stays honest

Independent views, polls and comments. Expired advice is greyed out and left out of the recommendation.

AI market brief

Key numbers, mill notes, risks and missing inputs, with a "good until" time so it never goes stale unnoticed.

Sales straight from your software

Sales flow in from the mill's own system, so sold quantities and averages are always current.
