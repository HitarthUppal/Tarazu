Privacy policy

Tarazu, Sugar Sale Intelligence System, powered by AgriMandi · Last updated 11 November 2026

Tarazu is a private business tool. It helps a sugar company's managers, owners and invited industry advisors decide when and at what price to sell sugar. It is provided by AgriMandi.live, a venture of JK India eAgriTech Limited, Mumbai, India ("we"), to its business clients. Accounts are created only by the client's administrator; there is no public sign-up.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, role, mobile number, email address (optional)To create your account, sign you in and send sign-in codes.
Your PINTo sign you in. Stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, including us, can read it.
What you enter: sale offers, views, comments, poll answers, decisions, sales recordsThis is the service: it is shown to the people in your company's Tarazu workspace according to their role.
Voice notes you recordSent once to our transcription service to turn speech into text. The text is saved; the audio is not kept after transcription.
Device push tokenTo send you notifications you have switched on.
Sign-in records: time, device type, approximate IP addressSecurity: to show your signed-in devices, detect misuse and keep an audit trail.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or browsing activity. Tarazu shows no advertising and we do not sell or rent personal data.

Who processes it for us

Our web hosting provider, Google Cloud (AI analysis and voice transcription, Gemini), n8n (workflow automation), Google Firebase and Apple (delivering push notifications), and, where enabled, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and an email provider (sign-in codes and notifications). They process data only to provide these functions.

How long we keep it

Business records are kept for as long as your company uses Tarazu, or as its agreement with us requires. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days without use. Encrypted backups are kept for 30 days. When your account is removed, your personal details are deleted; records you entered for your company (such as a decision or a sale) stay with your company, attributed to a removed user.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). PINs are hashed, sign-in sessions can be revoked at any time, sensitive actions are logged, and backups are encrypted. On phones, your session is kept in the device's secure storage and the app can lock behind Face ID or fingerprint.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Ask your company's Tarazu administrator, or write to us. You can also request account deletion at tarazu.live/delete-account.html.

Contact and grievances

Grievance Officer, JK India eAgriTech Limited, Mumbai, India · privacy@tarazu.live. We respond within 30 days.

Changes
